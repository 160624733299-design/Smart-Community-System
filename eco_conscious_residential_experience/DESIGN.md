## Brand & Style
This design system embraces an intentional Modern Organic Minimalist philosophy, paired with tactile digital clarity. It serves residents, facility managers, and eco-conscious homeowners of a forward-thinking gated residential community. 

The aesthetic communicates serenity, sustainable vitality, order, and effortless hospitality. It rejects clutter, over-saturated gradients, and harsh industrial edges. Instead, it balances open white airiness, verdant foliage-inspired botanical greens, and grounded mineral undertones. The overall experience must feel like walking through a sunlit, landscaped atrium: serene, organized, breathable, and deeply restorative.

## Colors
The palette derives from living botanical ecosystems and serene architectural stone:

- **Primary (`#1E7B4D`)**: A balanced, fresh leaf green embodying vitality, balance, and organic stewardship. Used for key call-to-actions, active navigation highlights, and high-priority states.
- **Secondary (`#0D9488`)**: Deep teal providing an intellectual, cool aquatic counterpart. Used for environmental metrics, secondary controls, utility indicators, and tertiary status tags.
- **Tertiary (`#10B981`)**: Radiant emerald green reserved for positive confirmations, active energy/water conservation achievements, verified badges, and toggle actives.
- **Neutral (`#475569`)**: Cool architectural slate. Provides high-legibility typographic hierarchy without the severe visual weight of pure black.

### Surface Tones & Backgrounds
- **Canvas Base**: `#F8FAF8` (A whisper of sun-bleached limestone and mist green).
- **Surface Elevation**: `#FFFFFF` (Crisp, clean elevated cards and interactive sheets).
- **Surface Variant / Track**: `#EDF3EE` (Muted organic mist used for form borders, inactive toggles, and dividers).
- **Text Emphases**: Primary text uses Slate 900 (`#0F172A`), secondary uses Slate 600 (`#475569`), and subtle metadata uses Slate 400 (`#94A3B8`).

## Typography
Plus Jakarta Sans delivers geometric precision softened by friendly, open apertures. It ensures immediate clarity in handheld, variable-lighting environments (e.g., bright outdoor community courtyards). 

- **Display & Large Headlines**: Tightly kerned with negative letter spacing to project modern architectural solidity.
- **Body Text**: Tuned with generous line heights to facilitate fluid scanning of notices, visitor logs, and utility metrics.
- **Labels & Pills**: Set in medium to semi-bold weights with slight letter spacing expansion to maintain crisp legibility at compact touch sizes.

## Layout & Spacing
A fluid 4-column layout governs compact viewports (mobile), scaling to an 8-column layout on tablets and a 12-column constrained grid for community dashboard displays.

- **Mobile Viewport Grid**: 4 columns, `1rem` margins, `1rem` gutters. All primary action surfaces scale fluidly across the device width.
- **Rhythm**: Anchored by an 8pt spatial grid. Component padding relies strictly on `space-sm` (8px) for compact tags, `space-md` (16px) for interior card content, and `space-lg` (24px) for separated card clusters.
- **Touch Ergonomics**: All interactive click targets mandate a minimum physical height of 48px to accommodate one-handed operation.

## Elevation & Depth
Elevation is expressed through ambient, soft-focus diffusion rather than stark vertical drop shadows. 

- **Surface Tiers**: Base screens rest on `#F8FAF8`. Layered cards exist on pristine `#FFFFFF` elevated with an organic, botanically tinted ambient shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(30, 123, 77, 0.05)`.
- **Active & Floating Elements**: Persistent floating action buttons (e.g., Quick Visitor Pass, SOS Security) use soft dual-layer diffusion: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(30, 123, 77, 0.16), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Low-Contrast Framing**: Card boundaries are defined by a whisper border of `1px solid rgba(71, 85, 105, 0.08)` to maintain structural integrity across screens without visual clutter.

## Shapes
The system uses rounded geometry (0.5rem base) to echo the curvature of river stones and architectural glass:

- **Cards & Modal Sheets**: `rounded-lg` (1rem / 16px) and `rounded-xl` (1.5rem / 24px), creating friendly, approachable surfaces.
- **Buttons & Inputs**: `0.75rem` (12px) to provide balanced, modern tactile clarity.
- **Badges, Tags, & Status Pills**: Complete pill radius (`9999px`) to immediately set metadata apart from actionable structural panels.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action**: 48px height, solid `#1E7B4D` background, text in white (`#FFFFFF`), `label-lg` typography, rounded to 12px. Active states transition smoothly to an emerald shade (`#155E3A`) with a subtle scale transform (`0.98`).
- **Secondary Action**: Translucent leaf-tinted container (`rgba(30, 123, 77, 0.08)`), text in `#1E7B4D`, borderless.
- **Ghost/Tertiary**: Clean outline with `1px solid rgba(71, 85, 105, 0.2)`, text in Slate 900 (`#0F172A`).

### Status Badges & Chips
- Fully pill-shaped (`border-radius: 9999px`), 28px height, horizontal padding of 12px.
- **Eco-Status / Positive**: Background `#ECFDF5`, text `#065F46`, paired with an optional 6px emerald status dot.
- **Pending / Utility**: Background `#F0FDFA`, text `#115E59`.
- **Neutral / Informational**: Background `#F1F5F9`, text `#334155`.

### Cards
- Pure white background (`#FFFFFF`), rounded to 16px (`rounded-lg`), padded with 16px or 20px. 
- Perimeter reinforced with `1px solid rgba(71, 85, 105, 0.06)` and ambient leaf-tinted drop shadows. Internal elements (such as amenity bookings, visitor entries, and solar yield data) are arranged with clean visual hierarchy.

### Form Inputs
- 48px height, rounded to 12px, border of `1.5px solid #E2E8F0`, surface of `#FFFFFF`.
- Focus state activates an outline in `#1E7B4D` alongside an exterior ring of `rgba(30, 123, 77, 0.15)`. Floating labels utilize `label-md` in `#475569`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- 20px size with a 6px radius for checkboxes, full circle for radios.
- Unchecked state: `#CBD5E1` border on pure white.
- Checked state: `#1E7B4D` solid fill displaying a crisp white micro-check or centered dot.

### Specialized Residential Components
- **Sustainability Metric Widget**: Card displaying solar generation or water consumption with an integrated mini-sparkline, leaf-tinted progress meters, and emerald achievement badges.
- **Gate Pass QR Plate**: High-contrast, clean white interior card with centered QR code, capped by an emerald pill timer indicating remaining pass validity.
